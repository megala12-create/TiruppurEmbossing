 3How is emboss different from the silicone HD option?
Emboss Printing and Silicone HD Printing are both used to achieve a raised finish, but they work in different ways. Emboss Printing uses heat and pressure to press the design directly into the fabric, creating a texture you can feel. It's a tone-on-tone finish, meaning the design is the same color as the fabric. Silicone HD Printing, on the other hand, applies high-density silicone inks to build a raised print surface on the garment. This process allows for a glossy, matte, or soft finish, and the design can be a different color from the fabric. So, the main difference is that Emboss Printing alters the fabric itself, while Silicone HD Printing adds a raised, flexible silicone layer on top of the fabric. Which aspect is more important to you: the tone-on-tone look or the ability to have a different color for the logo?

How is emboss different from the silicone HD option?
Emboss Printing and Silicone HD Printing are both used to achieve a raised finish, but they work in different ways. Emboss Printing uses heat and pressure to press the design directly into the fabric, creating a texture you can feel. It's a tone-on-tone finish, meaning the design is the same color as the fabric. Silicone HD Printing, on the other hand, applies high-density silicone inks to build a raised print surface on the garment. This process allows for a glossy, matte, or soft finish, and the design can be a different color from the fabric. So, the main difference is that Emboss Printing alters the fabric itself, while Silicone HD Printing adds a raised, flexible silicone layer on top of the fabric.
