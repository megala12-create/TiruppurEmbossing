Root causes behind the flagged answers

5 answers answers · Screen t8 & t10, Sticker t4, DTF t6 & t17

Invented specifics still slip through in about 3.6% of answers

The hard anti-fabrication rule stopped the obvious cases - asked point blank for maximum print size and silicone thickness, the assistant now refuses to guess, where before the fix it invented '40-45 cm'. But five answers still asserted specifics that exist nowhere in the business data: a 6-8 colour screen-printing capacity, the existence of a separate screen setup charge, waterproof/residue-free sticker vinyl, DTF having a softer hand than screen printing, and an offer of testing and reporting. The screen-printing pair are the serious ones, because a colour limit and a chargeable setup item are both things a buyer will plan and negotiate around.

Fix: Extend the prohibition beyond measurements to cover capability counts (how many colours/screens/layers) and the existence of any chargeable item, and add a closing self-check instruction: before sending, remove any number or chargeable item that did not come from the supplied knowledge. A cheap belt-and-braces addition is a server-side scan that strips or flags such claims before they reach the visitor.
